## 🕹️ About

OpenFront is a massively multiplayer real-time strategy game developed and published by OpenFront Inc. and Evan Pellegrini. Players begin with a small territory on a shared world map and compete with hundreds of others to expand, manage resources, build military infrastructure, form alliances, and dominate rival nations. Matches combine territorial control, economic planning, diplomacy, and real-time combat across maps based on real-world geography. OpenFront is currently in Early Access and includes solo play, team battles, free-for-all matches, ranked competition, and powerful weapons such as nuclear strikes.

OpenFront is for players who enjoy fast-paced grand strategy, territorial warfare, diplomacy, and competitive multiplayer battles.

## ✨ Features

- 🤝 **Alliances And Diplomacy** — Form alliances, coordinate offensives, and negotiate your way through shifting conflicts.
- 🌍 **Shared World Maps** — Compete across large maps inspired by real-world regions and continents.
- ☢️ **Strategic Weapons** — Deploy missiles, nuclear weapons, and defensive systems to change the course of battles.
- ⚔️ **Massive Real-Time Battles** — Fight alongside and against hundreds of players in a persistent shared battlefield.
- 🎯 **Multiple Game Modes** — Play solo scenarios, free-for-all matches, team games, and ranked battles.
- 💰 **Resource Management** — Balance gold, troop production, infrastructure, and military spending.
- 🏗️ **Territorial Expansion** — Capture land, establish borders, and push your front line toward enemy territory.
- 🗺️ **Accessible Strategy** — Learn the fundamentals through guided play while developing deeper tactical expertise.
